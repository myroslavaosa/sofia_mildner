Story

How it all began

Hi, I am Sofia Mildner – a trained educator (Erzieherin) and a certified vegan nutrition counsellor. Since I was 16 years old I have been fascinated by nutrition and body awareness, so I switched my own diet to vegan – step by step and entirely on my own.

After taking the pill for one year, I began to look consciously for hormone-free alternatives. I used to believe that the pill is the only reliable option there are several reliable, hormone-free methods. At the end of 2021 I stopped taking it. Through specialist literature, blogs and a lot of self-study I got to know my body and my cycle in a new, mindful way.

Today I live in harmony with my cycle and look forward to my period. I will show you how you, too, can use your cycle as a strength and practise hormone-free contraception.

— Sofia Mildner

From passion to profession

Alongside my training as an educator I turned my passion into my profession. In my coachings and workshops I accompany women who want to replace the idea “a period is something you just have to endure” with real understanding. Key topics are the NFP method, cycle-based nutrition and critical nutrients such as vitamin B12, iron and omega‑3 fatty acids.

As the poster on my desk says: Kind people build better worlds. A typical cycle lasts about 28 ± 7 days – and every phase (menstrual, follicular, ovulatory, luteal) needs something different.
That is exactly what we look at together.

Note: coaching complements but does not replace medical advice from a doctor.

What is NFP?

Natural Family Planning is a hormone-free method in which a woman observes body signs such as basal temperature and cervical mucus to determine her fertile and infertile days.
