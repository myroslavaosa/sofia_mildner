About Sofia

Sofia Mildner is a vegan nutrition counselor and a trained educator (Erzieherin) from Saxony, Germany. Born in 2004, she has been intensely interested in nutrition and body awareness since the age of 16, and she moved to a vegan diet on her own initiative.

At the end of 2021 she began to look more closely at her own health and stopped taking the contraceptive pill, which she had taken for about a year. Through specialist literature, blogs and a lot of self-study she learned to understand her body and cycle in a new, mindful way.

Alongside her training as an educator she turned that passion into a profession. In coachings and workshops she helps women look forward to their period and learn about hormone-free contraception.

Focus areas

  • Vegan nutrition for beginners and for everyday life
  • Vegan sports nutrition
  • Fasting and mindful eating
  • Women's health and cycle-based nutrition

Teaching

She teaches in Dresden at the Paracelsus Heilpraktikerschule, where she offers a seminar on understanding the natural cycle and on hormone-free contraception (NFP).

Ways of working

Talks, courses, workshops, one-to-one counseling and corporate health management, available on site, by video call, phone or e-mail.
