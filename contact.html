Let's get in touch

Whether you want to understand your cycle, switch to a vegan diet without nutrient gaps or learn hormone-free contraception – I am happy to hear from you. I work with women of every age and with companies that want to support the health of their teams.

Consultations take place on site in Coswig and Dresden, by video call, by phone or by e-mail. Workshops are held in small groups, so there is always time for your own questions. After registering you get access to recipes, cycle-tracking templates and dates of upcoming events.

Please use the forms below: register for workshops and online courses, sign in if you already have an account, or simply write a message. I usually answer within two working days.
