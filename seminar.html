Look forward to your period & use hormone-free contraception

About the seminar

Discover how to understand your natural cycle better and experience it in a positive way. In this seminar you will gain solid knowledge about female hormones, learn everyday methods to adapt your nutrition and handle stress, and get valuable insights into hormone-free contraception (NFP) – for more well-being and self-determination.

Programme

  1. Female hormones: oestrogen, progesterone & co.
  2. The four phases of the cycle and what they feel like
  3. Cycle-based nutrition on a plant-based diet
  4. Stress, sleep and the cycle
  5. Basics of NFP: temperature and cervical mucus
  6. Questions & personal exchange

Who is it for?

  • Women who want to stop or avoid hormonal contraception
  • Anyone who wants to feel better during their period
  • Practitioners in naturopathy and wellness
